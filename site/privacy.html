Privacy policy

cursorduck.com · last updated 23 September 2026 · Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

In short: this website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The duck extension itself never sends any data anywhere.

Who is responsible

Lucas Reiser, contact details in the legal notice.

Hosting

This website is delivered by Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver the pages and to protect them from attacks, Cloudflare processes the technical data of every request: your IP address, the requested address, date and time, browser type and the referring page. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a fast and secure website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Cloudflare keeps these logs only briefly; I do not have access to individual logs and keep no server logs of my own. Cloudflare acts as my processor under its data processing addendum. Transfers outside the EU are covered by the EU standard contractual clauses and Cloudflare's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Cloudflare's own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

What this site stores in your browser

A few preferences, kept in your browser's local storage and never transmitted: your language choice, your day-or-night choice, whether the duck's sounds are on, and for the wishing pond your voter id and the ideas you voted for. They are not cookies, contain no identifiers and you can delete them at any time through your browser's site data settings.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No embedded third-party content, no external fonts, no external scripts, with one exception: the bot check of the wishing pond, loaded only when you use it. Fonts and sounds are served from this domain.
  • No contact forms, no newsletter, no accounts.

The duck on this site

The duck swimming across this page is the extension's engine running locally in your browser. Like the extension, it reacts to your pointer, to keyboard activity in the demo field and to text selection, and it only ever learns where things are on screen, never what you type or select. Nothing leaves your browser.

The wishing pond

The idea board on the start page stores what you submit: the idea's title and text, its language and the time of submission. When you vote or submit for the first time, a bot check by Cloudflare Turnstile runs and the site then creates a random voter id for you. That id is kept in your browser's local storage together with the list of ideas you voted for, and every vote is stored as the pair of idea and voter id, so each device can toss one crumb per idea. The id is not linked to your name, e-mail address or IP address; I do not store IP addresses. Turnstile is loaded from Cloudflare only at that moment; during the check Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser characteristics under its own privacy policy. Submitted ideas are reviewed by me before they appear and may be shortened, declined or removed. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running the board and keeping bots out (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR); by submitting you agree to the publication of your text. If you want an idea removed, contact me with its text via the legal notice or GitHub.

External links

Links to GitHub (GitHub, Inc.), the Chrome Web Store (Google), Firefox Add-ons (Mozilla) and Microsoft Edge Add-ons (Microsoft) lead to services with their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow the link.

The extension

The browser extension collects no data and makes no network requests. Its full privacy policy, the one registered with all three stores, is published on GitHub: PRIVACY.md.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, the right to object and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since this site stores no personal data beyond the short-lived connection logs described above, there is usually nothing to hand over, but you are welcome to ask.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the legal notice or the GitHub profile forliHD.

Der Wunschteich

Das Ideenboard auf der Startseite speichert, was du einreichst: Titel und Text der Idee, ihre Sprache und den Zeitpunkt. Beim ersten Abstimmen oder Einreichen läuft ein Bot-Check von Cloudflare Turnstile, danach erzeugt die Seite eine zufällige Abstimm-Kennung für dich. Diese Kennung liegt zusammen mit der Liste der Ideen, für die du gestimmt hast, im lokalen Speicher deines Browsers; jede Stimme wird als Paar aus Idee und Kennung gespeichert, damit jedes Gerät pro Idee eine Krume werfen kann. Die Kennung ist nicht mit Name, E-Mail-Adresse oder IP-Adresse verknüpft; IP-Adressen speichere ich nicht. Turnstile wird nur in diesem Moment von Cloudflare geladen; während der Prüfung verarbeitet Cloudflare deine IP-Adresse und Browser-Merkmale nach seiner eigenen Datenschutzerklärung. Eingereichte Ideen prüfe ich, bevor sie erscheinen; sie können gekürzt, abgelehnt oder entfernt werden.
