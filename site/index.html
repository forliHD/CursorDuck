What she can do

Small duck, big repertoire.

Everything below happens on this page too, with the real engine. The dashed cards are for trying out.

🏊

Following

The farther away your cursor, the faster she paddles, wake and bow wave included. She turns into her swimming direction and sprints when she falls behind.

🫶

Petting

Let her catch up, then wiggle the cursor over her: squinty happy eyes, blushing cheeks, hearts. A full petting bar earns a happy quack.

Try it
🍞

Feeding

Double-click anywhere on this page to toss bread crumbs into the water. She swims over and picks them up one by one, and the ducklings join in. Just don't overfeed her.

Try it
📝

Reading along

Type into a text field and she watches the field instead of the cursor, and won't doze off. She only knows that you type, never what.

Try it
🍪

Selection snack

Select text near her and she nibbles at the edge of the selection. She sees where the text is, never what it says.

Go on, select this sentence with your mouse while she's close by. Nom.

Try it
🎵

Music & video

When a video or a song with sound plays on the page, she dances along and the ducklings bob to the beat. Every now and then a disco ball drops in and the whole family goes wild.

Two bars of chiptune, generated right here.
😴

Napping

After a while of quiet she tucks her head into her feathers. Zzz. When you're back she wakes up with a “!”. At night she gets sleepy sooner and dreams in little stars.

🐟

Fish hunt

Every now and then a fish shadow passes below the surface. She spots it, chases it, snaps, and doesn't always get it. The popup counts her catches.

💕

Visitors & shore leave

Every few minutes a wild duck swims by for a quack duet and a synchronized little dance. Now and then she climbs out of the water and waddles a proud circle across the page.

🐥

Ducklings

Up to six ducklings paddle in a row behind her, with their own little animations. When mama gets sleepy she tucks them into a nest first.

🎩

Wardrobe

14 hats and 5 pairs of glasses for any model, each unlocked by an achievement: top hat, crown, halo, ninja mask, sunglasses, monocle and more.

🏆

Achievements

60 badges in bronze, silver, gold and diamond, from petting fan through “Identity crisis” to the 30-day “Regular”. Every badge tells you how to earn it.

🙈

Focus mode & calm mode

On password fields and fullscreen videos she steps aside. With “reduce motion” enabled in your system she skips pirouettes, dancing and the disco ball.

📱

Touch

On touchscreens she follows your finger. Tap her to quack, double-tap to flap, double-tap the water to feed.

Privacy

Zero trackers. Zero cookies. Zero data.

The extension never talks to the internet. It doesn't know what you type or select, only where things are. This website plays by the same rules.

0network requests by the extension
0cookies and analytics on this site

The only things this site remembers are your language, your day-or-night choice and the ideas you tossed crumbs at, stored in your own browser. The wishing pond is the one exception to the no-third-party rule: the first time you vote or submit, Cloudflare’s Turnstile bot check loads.

Questions

Frequently quacked questions.

Does she slow down my browser?

No. She draws on her own canvas layer, detached from the page layout, pauses in background tabs and adapts her resolution on very busy pages.

She's in the way on one site.

Open the popup and choose “Pause on this site”. Alt+Shift+D toggles her everywhere, and the distance slider keeps her off the text you're reading.

Why is she asleep?

After a while without cursor movement she naps, anywhere from 5 to 60 seconds, adjustable in the popup. Move the mouse, scroll hard or play some music and she's back.

Does she see what I type?

Never. A key press is just an activity signal, a selection is just a rectangle on screen, and password fields hide her entirely.

What about Safari?

Not yet. She lives in Chrome, Firefox and Edge, and in Chromium browsers like Brave, Opera or Vivaldi via the Chrome Web Store.

Can I suggest a feature?

Please do. Toss it into the wishing pond right here on this site, or open an issue on GitHub.
